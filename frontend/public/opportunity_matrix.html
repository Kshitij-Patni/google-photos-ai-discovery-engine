How We Calculate the Opportunity Score

The Opportunity Score helps us decide what we should build or fix first. It ranks each problem on a 0-10 scale based on three ingredients:

Opportunity Score (0-10) = (Frequency Index × 0.4) + (Severity Index × 0.4) + (Feasibility × 0.2)

Here is what each component means in simple terms:

  • Frequency Index (How common is it?): The archetype's share of all relevant feedback, divided by the share of the most common archetype and scaled to 10. (Weight: 40%)
  • Severity Index (How painful is it?): Average user frustration (1-4) scaled to 10. A mild annoyance scores low, while an issue that makes users abandon the app scores high. (Weight: 40%)
  • Feasibility (How easy is it for us to fix?): Rated 0-10 for every archetype. A quick UI fix scores high, while inventing brand-new complex AI scores lower. (Weight: 20%)
